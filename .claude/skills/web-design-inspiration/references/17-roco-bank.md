# 17 · Roco.bank: digital banking card

**Family:** Bento Showcase · **Best for:** banking, cards, fintech, subscriptions, product feature showcases
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/25558499-Digital-Banking-Landing-Page-Website (Levi Wilson for QClay)
**Look at:** `images/17-roco-bank-hero.jpg` (light hero), `images/17-roco-bank-bento.jpg` (dark bento), `images/17-roco-bank-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Premium and tactile. Sculptural art on the cards, then a moody gallery of textures.

## Palette (roles)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Hero | `#FFFFFF` on a `#F2F2F2` frame |
| Ink | `#0D0D08`, black pill buttons |
| Card art | burnt orange `#E9783C` / `#B44B21`, sage `#6D8D49`, sand `#E4BD96` |
| Bento section | black `#0D0D0D` panel |

## Type
- Neo-grotesk with **mixed weights inside one line** ("Roco**.bank**", "Professional **bank** card", "AI **bank**", "Easy **pay**").
- Wide-tracked tiny uppercase eyebrows ("CREATE YOUR OWN", "PAY METHODS"). Money amounts in monospace.
- Free fonts: **Onest** or **Geist** (300 + 600) + **Geist Mono**.

## Layout & components
- **A fanned cascade of tilted 3D cards** with abstract sculptural art, spilling off the top-right of the hero.
- Huge vertical outlined text running up the right edge.
- Customer chips: avatar + name + mono amount in pill shapes, two of them tinted orange and green.
- Up/down arrow stepper on the left of the hero copy. Black "BUY CARD" + grey "DISCOVER" small pills. A partner-logo row.
- A dark rounded panel holding **a bento grid of photo-texture tiles** (rock, coral, folded ribbon, metal mesh), each carrying exactly one stat or feature ("+390k Customer to our bank card", "Invested $5.9m", "Payment with over 10 methods").

## Signature move
A fanned stack of tilted objects, plus texture-photo bento tiles that each hold one fact.

## Borrow
- Mixed-weight words as a consistent typographic accent.
- Bento tiles where the texture carries the mood and the text stays short.
- People-chips (avatar + name + value) as social proof.

## Don't copy
The Roco.bank name or logo, the card artwork, or the texture photos.
