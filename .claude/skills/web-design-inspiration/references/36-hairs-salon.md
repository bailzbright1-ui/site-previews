# 36 · Hair Salon Website

**Family:** Fashion Monochrome · **Best for:** hair salons, barbers, stylists, makeup artists, fashion photographers, models
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/24559400-Hair-Salon-Website (Ronas IT | UI/UX Team)
**Look at:** `images/36-hairs-salon-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Editorial, sharp and fashion-forward. A Vogue spread for a salon.

## Palette (roles)
strict black & white photography; white and black panels; dusty violet-grey #6B6A88 presentation background (optional brand tint); no other colour

## Type
tall condensed ALL CAPS display (ABOUT / SERVICES / GALLERY / STYLISTS) that overlaps photos; tiny sans body; wide-tracked 'F E M A L E / M A L E' labels; free: Bebas Neue or Oswald 500 + Inter

## Layout & components
hero split into two full-height B&W portraits (Female | Male) as the first choice; giant condensed section words with photos layered over part of the letters; services with tab chips (Color & Highlights / Cut & Styling) and a black full-width 'Book an appointment' bar; masonry B&W gallery on black; stylist profile with portrait, bio, 'Choose a specialist' bar and arrow carousel

## Signature move
Split-screen gender choice hero + condensed caps words partly covered by photos.

## Borrow
B&W-only photography rule for instant cohesion; first-click split hero for audiences with two paths.

## Don't copy
The model photos.
