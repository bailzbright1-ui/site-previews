# 21 · Landscaping Website Design

**Family:** Fresh Natural · **Best for:** landscaping, lawn care, gardening, mowing, tree services, outdoor living
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27807001-Landscaping-Website-Design (Yscale Studio)
**Look at:** `images/21-greenscape-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Lush, fresh and proud of the results. Green but not cliché.

## Palette (roles)
bright spring green field #8FD14F / #73B425 behind the page; soft sage-cream page #F0F6E9 / #D3D4B2; ink #0C0F03; ONE black CTA pill

## Type
huge bold rounded grotesk headline set over the photo ('Be Natural.') with a period; inline slash-separated service list in big light grey text with one service in ink; free: Bricolage Grotesque 700 or Manrope 800 + Manrope

## Layout & components
full-bleed aerial garden photo hero with the giant headline sitting on the bottom edge; small inset photo card top-left; about paragraph with highlighted words; four white stat tiles (1,200+ / 10+ / 1,200+ / 60+); 'Your outdoors is our specialty' with services as a slash-separated text list next to a photo; before/after slider 'Results speak for themselves'

## Signature move
Services written as one big slash-separated sentence where the active service turns ink.

## Borrow
Before/after slider for any visual trade; giant period-ended two-word headline over a hero photo.

## Don't copy
The GreenScape name or photos.
