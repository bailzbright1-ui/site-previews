# 68 · Wanderlust Travel Agency Website Design

**Family:** Retro Poster · **Best for:** travel agencies, tour operators, festivals, music venues, breweries, surf/skate brands
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/26015334--Wanderlust-Travel-Agency-Website-Design (Mike Taylor)
**Look at:** `images/68-wanderlust-travel-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Retro, adventurous and loud. A vintage travel poster.

## Palette (roles)
peach/salmon #FD915D page; dusty teal #2F5D6E panels with a sky-blue gradient #44B0DC fading in the hero; dark slate #0C1419 type; B&W nostalgic photos

## Type
ULTRA-TALL condensed display caps ('WANDERLUST', 'WHO WE ARE', 'FEATURED DESTINATIONS', 'CONTACT US') with landmarks (CN Tower, Statue of Liberty) rising THROUGH the letters; condensed caps body; free: Big Shoulders Display 800 or Antonio + Archivo Narrow

## Layout & components
hero word fills the width with a landmark photo cut-out overlapping it; search fields as condensed caps dropdowns (MADRID SPAIN / DUBLIN IRELAND / CHECK IN / CHECK OUT); 'SINCE 1975' badge card with B&W van photo; destination cards; each section headline is a giant condensed word

## Signature move
Landmark cut-outs rising through ultra-tall condensed words.

## Borrow
Heritage year ('since 1975') as a design element; condensed caps search bar.

## Don't copy
The Wanderlust name or photos.
