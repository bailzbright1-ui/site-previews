# 28 · Premium Car Detailing Website Design

**Family:** Clean Tech Editorial · **Best for:** car detailing, car wash, auto services, premium cleaning, product servicing
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27763841-Premium-Car-Detailing-Website-Design (Topnotch Studio)
**Look at:** `images/28-neowash-detailing-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Showroom-clean, precise and premium. Light instead of the usual dark car site.

## Palette (roles)
cool light grey #E0E5EA page; ink #080A0F; cobalt blue #273D90 / #0E2056 for the one highlight card, active dots and footer

## Type
neo-grotesk headings in two tones (ink + grey second half: 'Your Vehicle Deserves / More Than a Quick Wash.'); uppercase MONOSPACE micro copy for labels and paragraphs; free: Inter Tight + IBM Plex Mono

## Layout & components
hero with a classic car shot on light grey, headline top-right, a small cobalt 'Restore the Shine. Elevate the Drive.' card overlapping the photo; barcode-like hairline pattern strips as dividers; service cards with tag chips and 01/02/03 numbers + diagonal arrows; carousel with round arrows; giant 'SUBSCRIBE' word in the footer area

## Signature move
Two-tone headlines (second half in grey) + mono micro copy.

## Borrow
Light 'showroom' treatment for categories that usually go dark; hairline barcode divider pattern.

## Don't copy
The NeoWash name or photos.
