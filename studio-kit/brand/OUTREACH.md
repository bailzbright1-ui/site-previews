# Outreach

Every message has one job: get the owner to open the preview. Keep it short, personal and about their business. Replace every `{placeholder}`; never send one with a placeholder left in.

## First email

**Subject:** I built a website for {Business name}

> Hi {first name or "there"},
>
> I'm {your name} from One Sight Studio in {your suburb}. I came across {Business name} on {where you found them} and put together a website for you. It's already built. Have a look:
>
> {preview link}
>
> If you like it, it can be live on your own domain this week for $29 a month. No setup fee, no lock-in, and changes are included.
>
> If it's not for you, no worries. The preview comes down in 14 days.
>
> {your name}
> One Sight Studio · ABN {your ABN} · {mobile} · {email}
>
> Don't want emails from us? Reply "unsubscribe" and you won't hear from us again.

## Follow-up (once, 5–7 days later, only if no reply)

**Subject:** Re: I built a website for {Business name}

> Hi {first name or "there"}, just checking the link came through: {preview link}. Happy to change anything: photos, wording, colours. The preview is up until {date}.
>
> {your name} · One Sight Studio
> Reply "unsubscribe" to stop these emails.

## SMS (only to a mobile the business publishes for customers)

> Hi, {your name} from One Sight Studio here. I built a website for {Business name}, have a look: {preview link}. Live for $29/mo if you like it, no setup fee. Reply STOP to opt out.

## In person

Show the preview on your phone, then leave a card with the link and a QR code. Line to use: "I made you a website. Want to see it?" Then stop talking and hand them the phone.

## Rules for sending (Spam Act 2003)

Unsolicited marketing emails and texts are regulated in Australia. Follow all of these:

- **Consent.** Only message an email or mobile the business publishes for customers to contact them (their website, Google listing, Facebook page), and only about their business. Skip it if the listing says "no marketing" or similar.
- **Keep a record** of where and when you found each address (a column in your lead list is fine).
- **Identify yourself** in every message: your name, One Sight Studio, ABN and contact details.
- **Unsubscribe** line in every message, and honour it within 5 business days. No cost to unsubscribe.
- **No bought or scraped lists.** One message and one follow-up per business, then stop.
