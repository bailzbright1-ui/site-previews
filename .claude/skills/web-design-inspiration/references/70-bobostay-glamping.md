## Feel
Cosy, natural and escapist. Moody highlands and warm cottages.

## Palette (roles)
moody landscape greens and greys from photography; white content cards; spring-lime #B8E26A presentation backdrop; small orange dot accent

## Type
serif headlines mixing roman and ITALIC ('Nature Outside, *Comfort Inside*', 'Escape to Your Ideal Cottage Getaway — Peaceful, *Inviting*, and *Wrapped in Nature*'); free: Libre Baskerville or Lora (italic) + Inter

## Layout & components
full-bleed landscape hero with the cottage centred, headline top-left; glass card bottom-left with avatar stack '12k+ memberships'; short copy bottom-right; photo grids of cottages; testimonial quote with stars; 'Exclusive deals for your perfect getaway' cards

## Signature move
Italic emphasis woven through long, descriptive serif headlines.

## Borrow
Glass social-proof card on the hero photo; long, sensory headlines for hospitality.

## Don't copy
The BoboStay name or photos.
