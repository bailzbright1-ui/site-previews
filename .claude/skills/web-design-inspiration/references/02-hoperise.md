# 02 · HopeRise: charity landing page

**Family:** Warm Human · **Best for:** charities, health, community groups, education, any trust-first service
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/26290772-HopeRise-Charity-Landing-Page-Design (Phenomenon Labs for Phenomenon Studio)
**Look at:** `images/02-hoperise-hero.jpg` (desktop hero + donation form), `images/02-hoperise-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Warm, hopeful and human-first. Bold type, but soft surroundings.

## Palette (roles)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background | cream-to-sand vertical gradient `#F4EBDD` → `#EED4AC`, then white sections |
| Text | `#0D0B09` near-black |
| Action (only on Donate/primary) | `#2EA334` bright green |
| Supporting | `#B19C7A`, `#5A4434` warm browns for frames |

## Type
- Display: heavy, chunky grotesk, centred, Title Case, very tight leading (≈0.95).
- Body: friendly geometric sans, 14–16px.
- Free fonts: **Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold** or **Archivo Black** + **DM Sans**.

## Layout & components
- **A pill-shaped photo set inline inside the headline** ("Hope [photo] Rise / is Support").
- Primary green pill + a secondary text link with a circular arrow ("I need help ↗").
- Partner-logo strip directly under the hero, with "Quick Donate" on the left and "Scroll down" on the right.
- Three white rounded cards (Make a Donation / Get Support / Become a Volunteer). Each has a small circular icon, two lines of copy, a footer row (payment icons / hashtags / avatar stack) and a round arrow button.
- Donation widget: one-time/monthly toggle, amount input + currency, preset chips ($10/25/50/100), anonymous checkbox, terms checkbox, full-width green button.
- Story pages: circular portrait crops, fundraising progress bar, human headlines ("Help Anna...").

## Signature move
An inline photo inside the headline. It makes the hero feel personal straight away.

## Borrow
- One action colour that appears only on the thing you want clicked.
- Preset-amount chips (also good for quote/plan pickers).
- Card footers that carry proof (payment logos, avatars, tags).

## Don't copy
The HopeRise name, cancer-support copy, partner logos or real people's photos.
