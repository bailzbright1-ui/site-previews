# 03 · Vertex: real-estate developer

**Family:** Bold Colour Field · **Best for:** property developers, construction, architecture, investment
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27357269-Real-Estate-Website-Design-Vertex (Phenomenon Labs for Phenomenon Studio)
**Look at:** `images/03-vertex-hero.jpg` (notched hero + testimonials), `images/03-vertex-sheet.jpg` (spec bento, ROI calculator, mobile)

## Feel
Confident and architectural. A loud brand colour, offset by a calm sky and pastel cards.

## Palette (roles)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Brand field | safety orange ≈ `#FF6A13` (whole sections, highlighted tile, chart bars) |
| Sky | `#93BEDB` / `#6399BE` photographic sky blues |
| Pastel cards | butter `#FFF1C9`, ice `#DDEBFA`, blush `#FBE3E0` |
| Ink | `#1E0B09` |

## Type
- Display: condensed ALL-CAPS grotesk in a light-to-regular weight, stacked lines ("INVEST IN SPACES / THAT MATTER").
- Body: neutral sans, 14–16px.
- Free fonts: **Barlow Condensed** (400–500) or **Archivo** at `wdth 75` + **Inter**.

## Layout & components
- Hero photo sits inside a frame whose edges have **stepped, notched "jigsaw" cut-outs**. The top edge and lower corners carve into the image.
- Two small pill CTAs centred under the headline (outline "Book a call" + solid white "Invest").
- Tiny pixel-dot and asterisk glyphs used as decorative icons and section markers.
- Project page: bento of white spec tiles (Completion date, Status, Site area, Type, Developer, Starting price) with one tile filled orange, plus a floor-plan thumbnail.
- Interactive ROI calculator with sliders and an orange bar chart.
- Star-rated testimonial cards in a horizontal carousel that fades at both edges.

## Signature move
Notched image masks (CSS `clip-path` / SVG mask) plus one tile flooded with the brand colour.

## Borrow
- Spec facts as a bento of small labelled tiles instead of a bullet list.
- An interactive calculator as a lead magnet (pricing, savings, ROI).
- A decorative glyph system made from tiny pixel or asterisk shapes.

## Don't copy
The Vertex name or "V" mark, the woven-facade building photos, or the exact notch pattern.
