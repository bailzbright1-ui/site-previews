# 06 · Axon Labs: AI financial intelligence platform

**Family:** Engineered Blueprint · **Best for:** B2B SaaS, data, fintech, dev tools, engineering firms
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27848306-AI-Financial-Intelligence-Website (Zajno)
**Look at:** `images/06-axon-hero.jpg`, `images/06-axon-sheet.jpg` (frames from the scroll video)

## Feel
Precise and engineered. Like a technical drawing that became a website.

## Palette (roles)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Canvas | cool light grey `#E4E4E4` / `#BCBABB` |
| Panels | graphite `#2B2B2B`, ink `#070606` |
| Accent | hot orange `#DF4118`, used as tiny squares, active states and one slanted band |

## Type
- Display: **wide / extended** geometric ALL CAPS ("AXON STARTS WHERE SPREADSHEETS STOP").
- Labels: small uppercase with `//` suffixes ("CONNECTS TO YOUR STACK //").
- Free fonts: **Archivo** at `wdth 125` (SemiBold) or **Unbounded** + **IBM Plex Mono** / **Inter**.

## Layout & components
- Hairline grid cells sit behind the headline, with 45° diagonal hatch stripes and crosshair registration lines.
- **Isometric line-drawn cubes** with orange faces illustrate the data flow.
- A numbered vertical tab rail (01–04) on the left, with the active tab as a solid black block.
- An integration logo marquee in a bordered strip, with a footer row ("BROWSE INTEGRATIONS →" plus compliance badges in tiny caps).
- Three process tiles (Ingest / Attribute / Connect) with pixel-diagram illustrations.
- Black "REQUEST DEMO" block button with an orange left edge.
- Footer: a giant grey wordmark on graphite.

## Signature move
A blueprint layer of grids, hatching and isometric line art, with one orange accent.

## Borrow
- Technical-drawing decoration for anything engineered (SVG patterns, not images).
- A numbered tab rail for multi-step product explanations.
- Compliance and trust facts written as tiny caps under CTAs.

## Don't copy
The Axon name or logo, or the exact cube illustrations.
