# 74 · Beyond Architecture Firm Website Design

**Family:** Playful 3D · **Best for:** builders, architects, property developers, home loans, kids-friendly or approachable construction brands
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/26667533-Beyond-Architecture-Firm-Website-Design (Rylic Studio)
**Look at:** `images/74-beyond-architecture-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Optimistic, playful and colourful. Toy-like 3D buildings and sunny skies.

## Palette (roles)
sky blue #5FDCFE / #20C3F9 skies; deep navy #040614 / #0D1B4D frames and footer; orange #F06A2B pill CTAs; butter yellow #F5D66B and pastel tiles

## Type
bold rounded grotesk headlines with tiny INLINE 3D ICONS between words ('Building dreams, one design [icon] at a time', 'Design beyond [icon] boundaries [icon] creating possibilities'); a handwritten signature; free: Plus Jakarta Sans 800 + Inter

## Layout & components
hero tile: 3D-render house in a cartoon sky with a plane and sun, headline + orange pill; navy side panel with a tall tower and project name; architect profile card with signature; pastel tiles holding tower cut-outs; line-drawing building sketch next to stats (60%); giant footer wordmark with inline image tiles

## Signature move
Tiny 3D icons inserted between words in headlines.

## Borrow
Line-sketch + finished-render pairing to show 'design to reality'; inline emoji-like 3D icons (use sparingly).

## Don't copy
The Beyond name or renders.
