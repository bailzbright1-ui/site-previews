# 16 · Luxe Escape: boutique hotel

**Family:** Quiet Luxury · **Best for:** hotels, short stays, venues, spas, restaurants with bookings
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/25790336-Hotel-Website-Design-Concept (Ronas IT | UI/UX Team)
**Look at:** `images/16-luxe-escape-hero.jpg` (full page overview), `images/16-luxe-escape-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Warm, grown-up hospitality. Crisp UI with amber-lit wood interiors.

## Palette (roles)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Page | `#FCFCFC` |
| Panels | warm stone `#EDEDE7` |
| Buttons | `#000000` pills |
| Photo warmth | `#593821`, `#8C6247`, `#9B8973` |

## Type
- **Inter only.** Big regular-weight UPPERCASE headings, **with the second line indented to the right** ("WELCOME TO / ____LUXE ESCAPE"). Small sentence-case body.
- Free fonts: **Inter 400** (or **Inter Tight**).

## Layout & components
- Intro: the staggered headline, with copy on the left and a black "Book now" pill aligned right on the same row.
- A full-width lobby photo directly under the hero text.
- "Our Rooms" bento of mixed-size photos. One grey text tile holds its own "Book now" CTA. Round arrow buttons, tiny "1 bed | 2 sleeps" meta with ↗ icons.
- "Visualize our modern hotel" video tile with a white play circle.
- Booking bar: underlined Check-in / Check-out / Guests fields + a black "Apply" pill, then results as a photo grid.
- Restaurant split card: a text tile with "Reservation" + a photo carousel.

## Signature move
The staggered indented headline, plus a bento that mixes photo tiles with one text-and-CTA tile.

## Borrow
- Indented second lines for headings (simple, elegant, very reusable).
- Booking fields as minimal underlined inputs.
- A text tile inside an image bento.

## Don't copy
The Luxe Escape name or the hotel photos.
