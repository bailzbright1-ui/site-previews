Meet Pete

A local family connection.

Pete has nearly 20 years of plumbing experience, and ran his own plumbing business in Brisbane for over 12 years before starting Mount Coolum Plumbing in 2023.

His family's history in Coolum goes back to the 1930s. His dad's family home stood where the Clubb Coolum Resort is now; his dad was a cadet at the Coolum Surf Club at 14, later Captain, and is still a Life Member. His grandfather farmed bananas and sugar cane locally, and his grandmother was the principal (and only teacher) of Coolum Primary School in the early 1940s.

Now Pete and his family live in Mount Coolum, and his kids play on the same school grounds their grandad did 70 years ago. Keeping to a small area means he can get to your job quickly.
