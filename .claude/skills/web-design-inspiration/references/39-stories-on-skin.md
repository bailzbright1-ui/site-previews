# 39 · Landing Page Tattoo studio Website design

**Family:** Brutal Mono + Electric · **Best for:** tattoo studios, piercing, streetwear, gyms, music venues, skate shops
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/24563763-Landing-Page-Tattoo-studio-Website-design (Sahil Dobariya)
**Look at:** `images/39-stories-on-skin-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Raw, bold and inked. Street-poster energy.

## Palette (roles)
black and white with B&W skin photography; ONE muted teal #2E8C86 used as slashed shapes behind portraits and as label chips

## Type
ultra-condensed heavy caps (A TOUCH OF INK, NEW TATTOO DESTINE, ETERNAL TATTOO) with photos cutting through the letters; vertical rotated caps text; free: Anton or Oswald 700 + Inter

## Layout & components
black hero with two B&W portraits on teal slashes and the giant condensed headline overlapping the bottom of the photos; white sections with huge pale ghost letters behind; style list as teal/black label chips (BLACKWORK / DOTWORK / REALISTIC); vertical 'ETERNAL TATTOO' running beside a gallery column

## Signature move
Photo subjects breaking through giant condensed letters.

## Borrow
Knock-out headlines with people overlapping; tag chips for styles/services.

## Don't copy
The Stories on Skin name or photos.
