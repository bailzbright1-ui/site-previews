# 66 · Pet Care Website

**Family:** Playful 3D · **Best for:** pet care, groomers, vets, doggy daycare, pet shops, kids' services
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/15494451-Pet-Care-Website (Orizon: UI/UX Design Agency)
**Look at:** `images/66-pups-pet-care-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Cheerful, colourful and caring.

## Palette (roles)
white; sky-blue #0F9FFE blob and sunshine yellow circle behind the hero subject; violet #463AA4 CTA and active card; dark navy #1C1B2E service strip; orange #D68A32 accents

## Type
high-contrast chunky SERIF headline in navy ('Your Pet care center'); small sans; free: DM Serif Display or Recoleta-like Fraunces 700 + DM Sans

## Layout & components
hero subject (person + dog) cut out over a large blue blob and yellow circle; small floating circular pet avatars around; mini cards (Pet Health blog card, 'How to take care of your pets' video card); dark rounded services strip with pet cut-outs and one violet raised card; paw-print watermarks

## Signature move
Cut-out subject over overlapping blob + circle shapes with floating avatar bubbles.

## Borrow
Floating circular avatars to suggest community; dark service strip with one raised highlighted card.

## Don't copy
The Pup's name or photos.
