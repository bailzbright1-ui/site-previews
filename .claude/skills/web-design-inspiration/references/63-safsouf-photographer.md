# 63 · Luxury Photographer Thank You Page

**Family:** Moody Editorial · **Best for:** wedding photographers, videographers, celebrants, bridal stylists, florists, luxury events
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27307962-Luxury-Photographer-Thank-You-Page (Zane Krumanocker)
**Look at:** `images/63-safsouf-photographer-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Romantic, film-grain and intimate. Handwritten and tactile.

## Palette (roles)
muted sage-grey #8C8A79 / olive-black #181A15 photography tones; warm paper envelope #E9DCD2; white type; no bright colours

## Type
thin wide-tracked caps nav, an elegant serif wordmark, and a HANDWRITTEN script message on a card ('Received with thanks, we'll connect within the next day or two!'); free: Cormorant Garamond + Pinyon Script / Mrs Saint Delafield + Josefin Sans

## Layout & components
framed film photograph panel over a misty forest background; nav links spread evenly across the top with a centred serif wordmark; a deckle-edged paper card in an envelope as the confirmation message; small outlined 'Return to home' button

## Signature move
Form-confirmation page as a physical letter in an envelope.

## Borrow
Design the thank-you/confirmation page as part of the brand experience; paper textures for wedding/event businesses.

## Don't copy
The studio name or photos.
