# 51 · Brand Illustrations for Ora s Garden website

**Family:** Illustrated Craft · **Best for:** jams, sauces, honey, farm produce, gift shops, artisan food, garden centres
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/26530955-Brand-Illustrations-for-Ora-s-Garden-website (Daria Lada)
**Look at:** `images/51-oras-garden-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Handmade, charming and delicious. A storybook pantry.

## Palette (roles)
parchment page #EDE3CF framed by a per-product colour (burnt orange #B44E1E for apricot jam, forest #34503F for pesto, sage-grey for tomato); ink #20170A

## Type
playful script/display serif for product names ('Golden dawn', 'Basil bliss', 'Tomato basil'); typewriter MONO for nav, labels and copy ('(New in Store)'); free: Shrikhand or Yeseva One + Courier Prime

## Layout & components
centred product jar surrounded by botanical ILLUSTRATIONS of its ingredients; frame colour changes per product as you scroll; product switcher list on the left with the active item bulleted; round arrow button on the right; weight options under the product

## Signature move
Product surrounded by hand-drawn ingredient illustrations, frame colour themed per product.

## Borrow
Per-product colour theming; illustrations instead of photos for small producers without a photoshoot.

## Don't copy
The illustrations or brand name.
