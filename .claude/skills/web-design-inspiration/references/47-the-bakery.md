# 47 · Bakery Website Shopify Store Design

**Family:** Friendly Local Service · **Best for:** bakeries, delis, butchers, farm shops, home-made food businesses
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/19997471-Bakery-Website-Shopify-Store-Design (Mike Taylor)
**Look at:** `images/47-the-bakery-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Warm, wholesome and airy. Fresh bread on white linen.

## Palette (roles)
white page; warm crust browns from photography; terracotta-caramel #C8783B CTA buttons and headings; charcoal #3B3B3B secondary buttons

## Type
huge VERTICAL brand word rotated 90° down the left edge ('Bakery'), clean humanist sans headings in caramel; free: Archivo Black for the vertical word + Lato / Nunito Sans

## Layout & components
product cut-out (bread basket + wheat) as hero image with lots of white space; thin circular line arcs framing round product cut-outs; 'How it's made' with ingredient icons; chef cut-out portraits inside circular strokes; testimonials; contact form beside a cookie cut-out

## Signature move
Giant brand word set vertically along the left edge.

## Borrow
Cut-out food on white with thin circle strokes; vertical type for a narrow-format hero.

## Don't copy
The photos.
