## Feel
Old-world heritage told with modern precision. It's slow and sensory, and the photography carries the mood.

## Palette (roles)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Base / panels | `#100C05` olive-black (UI panels float over photography) |
| Paper | `#E8E6D5` warm cream (editorial panels, page edges) |
| Accent | `#D7A84A` muted gold (used sparingly) |
| Supporting | `#523117` cask brown, `#768965` vine sage, `#C5BDA4` parchment |

## Type
- Display: high-contrast condensed **italic serif**, stacked short sentences with full stops ("Wine. Intelligence. Intuition.").
- Body: small, clean grotesk (13–15px), generous leading.
- Free fonts for the same feel: **Instrument Serif Italic** or **Gloock** with **Instrument Sans** / **Inter**.

## Layout & components
- Dark UI panels (nav, product info) sit on top of full-bleed, sunlit vineyard photos. Cream panels hold editorial copy.
- A giant ghosted italic word ("exclusive", "about us") sits as a watermark behind sections at ~6% opacity.
- Catalogue: bottle cut-outs on dark tiles, 4-column grid, filter row above (taste notes, occasion, vineyard, vintage).
- Product page: bottle left, serif product name right, price, meta list, quantity stepper + outlined "Add to Cart", **characteristics rated with 5-dot scales**, then "The Story".
- AI sommelier chat: greeting, pill suggestion chips ("Find the Right Wine", "Ask a Sommelier", "A dish I'm planning"), input with mic.
- Small italic sign-offs in corners ("est. 1988").

## Signature move
Italic serif plus a giant ghost-word watermark over warm photography. It feels like a printed wine label brought to life.

## Borrow
- Dot-scale ratings for any sensory or spec attribute (strength, difficulty, intensity).
- Chips-first conversational widgets instead of an empty chat box.
- UI panels layered over photography, rather than photos boxed inside the UI.

## Don't copy
The VineWinner name, the vineyard and bottle imagery, "est. 1988", or the exact product-page arrangement.
