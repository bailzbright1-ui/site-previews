# 38 · Stive Hair Salon Website Design

**Family:** Colour-Block Collage · **Best for:** hair salons, interior studios, galleries, boutique retail, architects
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27874058-Stive-Hair-Salon-Website-Design (Jenny Marsden)
**Look at:** `images/38-strive-hair-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Fresh, architectural and stylish. Colour blocks like a modern interior.

## Palette (roles)
white page; ONE saturated cobalt #0916CC as big rectangular blocks behind photos; charcoal #3A3D44 text panels; photography brings warm skin tones

## Type
geometric sans, wide-tracked caps for the brand ('STRIVE HAIR'), caps statement 'CUT. COLOUR. CREATE'; free: Josefin Sans or Montserrat 600 (tracking 0.12em) + Montserrat

## Layout & components
collage hero: a tall cobalt rectangle behind, a charcoal caption panel overlapping it, a portrait photo and an interior photo offset at different heights; following sections repeat the cobalt block in a stepped zig-zag down the page with photos and short caps headings (THE SALON / HAIR) + black square CTA buttons

## Signature move
A single colour block that zig-zags down the page, with photos and text panels clipped onto it.

## Borrow
One geometric brand shape as the page's backbone connecting sections.

## Don't copy
The Strive name or photos.
