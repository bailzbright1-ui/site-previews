# 43 · Pilates Studio Website Design

**Family:** Swiss Scale Contrast · **Best for:** pilates, boutique gyms, studios, fashion brands, personal brands, salons
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27439581-Pilates-Studio-Website-Design (Ismael Monroy)
**Look at:** `images/43-kova-pilates-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Confident, boutique and editorial. A fashion campaign for a studio.

## Palette (roles)
deep forest-black #0F1A14 / #080805 hero; warm stone #E2DCCE content sections; tiny lime #D4F530 square on the CONTACT button; white type

## Type
colossal geometric sans brand word with a © symbol across the bottom of the hero ('KOVA ©'); small sentence-case copy; numbered class list 01 / 02; free: Syne 700 or Unbounded 600 for the wordmark + Inter

## Layout & components
full-bleed cinematic portrait hero with short copy left and right and the giant wordmark overlapping the subject; 'Pilates the way it was meant to be practiced.' editorial split with an offset photo; 'Three principles that guide everything we do' three-photo row; classes as numbered rows (01 Reformer Fundamentals) with photo + outline buttons; testimonial cards rail

## Signature move
Giant brand wordmark with © across a cinematic portrait.

## Borrow
Treat a small business brand like a fashion label: one portrait, one giant word, very little else.

## Don't copy
The KOVA name or photos.
