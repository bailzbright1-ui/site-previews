# 08 · Lumen: digital marketing agency

**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/25900970-Lumen-Landing-Page-Design-for-Marketing-Agency and https://dribbble.com/shots/25520596-Lumen-Marketing-Agency-Landing-Page-Design (Phenomenon Labs for Phenomenon Studio, the same project posted twice)

## Feel
Dark, glossy and expensive-tech. The light refracts through everything.

## Palette (roles)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Base | `#050605` / green-black `#060807` |
| Art | iridescent / prismatic liquid-chrome 3D forms (rainbow-edged refraction) |
| Accent | vivid orange ≈ `#FF5A1F` (CTA pills, service labels) |
| Cards | `#F7F9F7` white rounded cards on the dark page |

## Type
- Display: neo-grotesk ALL CAPS, tight leading, medium weight.
- **Staggered hero**: line 1 left-aligned, lines 2–3 indented to the right half ("ILLUMINATE YOUR / ____MARKETING / ____FUTURE").
- Free fonts: **Inter Tight 500** (uppercase, `-0.03em`) or **Schibsted Grotesk** + **Inter**.

## Layout & components
- Nav: a tiny logo top-left, with nav links stacked vertically in the top-right corner.
- Service sections alternate left and right copy blocks over the 3D art.
- White rounded service cards with tiny orange icon tags (Keyword research / On-page / Technical audits).
- Orange text-only sub-service lists (AUDIENCE TARGETING / ROI TRACKING).
- Testimonial carousel of white cards on a grey gradient, with round arrow buttons.
- **A colossal brand wordmark in a chrome/silver gradient, cropped at the bottom of the viewport** and overlapping section edges.

## Signature move
Iridescent chrome art on black, plus a giant metallic wordmark that bleeds off the bottom.

## Borrow
- Staggered multi-line headlines across a 2-column grid.
- One accent colour reserved for CTAs on an otherwise colourless page.
- A footer wordmark as a closing visual.

## Don't copy
The Lumen name or mark, or the specific chrome ribbon renders. Make your own abstract art (CSS gradients, WebGL or commissioned images).
