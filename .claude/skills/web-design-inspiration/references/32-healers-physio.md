## Feel
Restorative, grounded and expert. Calm green with energy.

## Palette (roles)
deep forest green #0E2A22 hero and bands; warm stone page #D4D2C5; mint/spring green #33D151 for CTAs, full stops and the ribbon; ink #060908

## Type
high-contrast SERIF headings ('Heal. Strengthen. Thrive.' with green full stops); giant serif stat numeral '12k'; small sans body; free: DM Serif Display or Young Serif + Inter

## Layout & components
dark green hero with three one-word sentences and a framed photo of a physio session; huge '12k + Satisfied Clients' numeral next to an intro paragraph; a wavy mint ribbon carrying the service names as curved text (Sports Rehabilitation · Post-Surgery Recovery · Geriatric Physiotherapy); 'Why choose' row alternating photo tiles and mint/blue-grey icon tiles; dark testimonial cards; tips/blog cards; slanted dark CTA 'Ready to Start Your Recovery?'

## Signature move
Wavy ribbon with services set on a curved text path.

## Borrow
Curved-text ribbon divider (SVG textPath); coloured full stops in headlines.

## Don't copy
The Healers name or photos.
