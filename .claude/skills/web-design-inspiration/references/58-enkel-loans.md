# 58 · Loan Mortgage Website

**Family:** Bento Showcase · **Best for:** mortgage brokers, finance, insurance quotes, solar savings, any quote-calculator business
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/24723037-Loan-Mortgage-Website (Dstudio Agency)
**Look at:** `images/58-enkel-loans-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Fresh, optimistic and simple. Making a dull product feel easy.

## Palette (roles)
white; lime-to-lilac soft gradient wash behind the hero; lime #C6EF6E, lilac #C9A6F4 and teal #3BA9B6 as step-card and chart fills; black pill buttons

## Type
elegant high-contrast SERIF for big headlines and huge numerals ('Completely Hassle-Free Process.', '$78.2M'); clean sans for UI; free: Fraunces 400 or Instrument Serif + Inter

## Layout & components
hero with headline, black 'Get Started' + split-button 'Chat with experts', tick reassurances; LOAN CALCULATOR card on the right (amount, gradient slider, 'Start Now', trust row); 'Loan Success Till Today — $78.2M' with a growth chart built as coloured columns per year; product cards with UI overlays on photos; three colourful 'How it works' step cards

## Signature move
Bar chart made of full-height coloured columns as a section's design.

## Borrow
Instant calculator in the hero (quote estimator for trades, savings estimator for solar); serif for numbers.

## Don't copy
The brand name or photos.
