## Feel
Punchy and confident. A trade business with personality.

## Palette (roles)
electric blue gradient hero (#2F55D4 to #9DB8F2); pale lime panels #E3F3A1; ink #0A0A0D; orange pill #E8692E; pastel step cards (lilac, ice, peach)

## Type
condensed heavy ALL CAPS (RIDGELINE ROOFING, OUR PROCESS, BUILT TO LAST.) + rounded geometric sans for lists; free: Anton or Archivo Black (wdth 75) + Poppins/Outfit

## Layout & components
hero card with blue gradient and a big rounded photo overlapping, giant condensed name along the bottom edge; centred service list in a lime rounded panel with one service as a tilted orange sticker; 01/02/03 process cards with photo + coloured big number + lime arrow button; 'BUILT TO LAST.' split with stats 12K+ / 25+

## Signature move
Service list where the highlighted service is a tilted orange sticker.

## Borrow
Pastel-tinted numbered process cards; condensed caps statements as section openers.
