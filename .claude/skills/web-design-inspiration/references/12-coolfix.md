# 12 · CoolFix: AC repair service

**Family:** Soft Atmospheric Service · **Best for:** trades and home services (AC, plumbing, lawn care, cleaning, pest control), local businesses
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/26173049-CoolFix-Repair-Service-Website-Design (Phenomenon Labs for Phenomenon Studio)
**Look at:** `images/12-coolfix-sheet.jpg` (hero, stats, mobile), `images/12-coolfix-detail.jpg` (services list + 5 steps up close)

## Feel
Fresh, calm and trustworthy. The brand promise ("cool air") is the visual atmosphere.

## Palette (roles)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Sky | `#5D9DD0` (primary), `#95CADF`, `#B4D4E3`, with cloud and mountain photography |
| Deep | navy `#0B243B` (text on light, testimonial card) |
| Base | white, pale grey `#F3F5F7` step cards |

## Type
- Friendly geometric sans, semibold, Title Case headings ("Everything Your AC Could Ever Need").
- **Stat numerals LARGE and LIGHT in blue** (10 / 1,500+ / 4.9 / 30) with tiny uppercase captions.
- Eyebrows: "● OUR SERVICES".
- Free fonts: **Plus Jakarta Sans** (200 for numerals, 600 for headings) or **General Sans** (Fontshare).

## Layout & components
- The hero sits over sky photography: headline centred, solid white "Book a Repair" pill + outline "Get Estimate" pill.
- A giant white wordmark behind the device mockups.
- **White clay-render 3D floor plan** with a blue pulse where the AC unit is (explains the service visually).
- Services as a vertical list with a blue left rule. The active item expands with a filled blue arrow circle.
- **"From Call To Cool In 5 Steps"**: a row of five step cards. The first is filled blue with copy, the rest pale grey with small dot-glyph icons and numbered caps labels.
- Dark testimonial card with a customer photo.
- Phone + "Book a Repair" always in the header.

## Signature move
Atmosphere as metaphor: the hero imagery is the feeling the service delivers. Add a clean 3D diagram of where the service happens.

## Borrow (very transferable to any trade business)
- Pick a sensory metaphor for the outcome (fresh air → sky; neat lawn → striped grass from above; clean → light and glass).
- Numbered step cards for the booking process, with the current step filled.
- Large light numerals for trust stats.
- A sticky header CTA + phone icon.

## Don't copy
The CoolFix name or snowflake mark, or the AC unit renders.
