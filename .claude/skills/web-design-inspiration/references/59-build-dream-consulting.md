photo hero card with stats chips (10+ / 250+ / 40%) and the statement over the photo; dark grid of numbered service cards; 'Who we are' with portrait; why-us icons; project photo row; FAQ list; footer with logo

## Signature move
Copper gradient glow bleeding from the page edges into a dark layout.
