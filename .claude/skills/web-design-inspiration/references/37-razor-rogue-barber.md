# 37 · Barbershop Website Design

**Family:** Moody Editorial · **Best for:** barbershops, tattoo studios, cocktail bars, whisky/cigar lounges, menswear
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27275652-Barbershop-Website-Design (Muhammad Shofiuddoula)
**Look at:** `images/37-razor-rogue-barber-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Old-world gentleman's club, but with modern editorial type.

## Palette (roles)
near-black #070605 and warm walnut photo tones; antique gold/tan #A79073 type; pale stone #D8CCB5 panels for contrast sections

## Type
high-contrast DIDONE / fashion serif in ALL CAPS with dramatic stagger and indent ('CRAFTING / ____TIMELESS / LOOKS FOR / THE MODERN / ____GENTLEMAN'), words split across lines ('GA / LL / ERY'); free: Bodoni Moda or Playfair Display SC + Inter

## Layout & components
dark panels framed over a blurred barbershop video background; giant stacked serif statements; gallery page with letters broken over three lines beside a portrait; thin circular hairline arcs as decoration; small photo insets beside headings

## Signature move
Words broken across lines in huge serif caps ('GA-LL-ERY').

## Borrow
Video-blur background behind framed content panels; staggered serif caps statements.

## Don't copy
The Razor & Rogue name or footage.
