# 10 · Pretty Patty: neighbourhood burger joint

**Family:** Loud Pop · **Best for:** restaurants, cafes, food trucks, local retail, fun consumer brands
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27154673-Restaurant-Modern-Website-Design-Pretty-Patty (Phenomenon Labs for Phenomenon Studio)
**Look at:** `images/10-pretty-patty-hero.jpg` (menu section), `images/10-pretty-patty-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Retro diner energy. Hand-made and fun, but still disciplined and well-aligned.

## Palette (roles)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Brand field | cobalt `#2A5BD7` / deep `#0B289F` |
| Page | warm cream `#F6EFDD` / `#E9E3D3` |
| Headline on blue | mustard `#FBA516` |
| Prices & arrows | tomato `#D15223` |

## Type
- Display: heavy, slightly condensed caps with soft corners, sometimes set on a slight tilt.
- Body & ingredient lists: **uppercase typewriter monospace**, with key words underlined.
- Free fonts: **Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold** (condensed width) or **Dela Gothic One** + **Courier Prime** / **Space Mono**.

## Layout & components
- **Background bands of giant repeating outlined slogan text running diagonally** ("FRIENDLY, NEIGHBORHOOD BURGER JOINT · LOCAL, FRESH...").
- Blue bathroom-tile grid backdrops (a CSS grid-line pattern).
- Product cut-outs with **circular "sticker" badges** per item.
- Arched and oval photo crops in a row (About section).
- Menu carousel: the centre item is large, with neighbours smaller and red round arrow buttons. Each item lists INGREDIENTS and SINGLE / DOUBLE / TRIPLE prices in red.
- A tiny icon-button pair (delivery apps) next to an "ORDER NOW" pill in the header.

## Signature move
Diagonal outlined slogan bands, plus sticker badges on product cut-outs.

## Borrow
- Slogan bands as section dividers (CSS `transform: rotate(-8deg)` + `-webkit-text-stroke`).
- Tile, grid or texture patterns made in CSS that echo the physical venue.
- Mono-caps for practical info (ingredients, specs, hours).

## Don't copy
The Pretty Patty name, burger photos, sticker artwork or slogan text.
