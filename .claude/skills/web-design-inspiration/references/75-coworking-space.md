# 75 · Coworking Space Website Template for WordPress

**Family:** Swiss Scale Contrast · **Best for:** coworking spaces, studios for hire, gyms, function rooms, offices, business centres
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/24431778-Coworking-Space-Website-Template-for-WordPress (Slider Revolution)
**Look at:** `images/75-coworking-space-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Grounded, practical and warm-modern. Concrete, wood and an orange chair.

## Palette (roles)
warm putty #EDE6DA / stone #D6D2C8 page; espresso #3A2418 type; safety orange #F5822A for arrow buttons, the testimonial panel and the menu panel

## Type
heavy grotesk ALL CAPS, tight leading ('DISCOVER YOUR IDEAL WORKSPACE', 'JOIN OUR COMMUNITY TODAY!'); small sans; free: Archivo Black or Inter Tight 800 + Inter

## Layout & components
hero with a giant caps headline overlapping a cropped interior photo; small orange square arrow buttons; four stats with orange numbers on hairlines (1,500+ Happy Members / 20,000+ Coffee Cups / 250+ Events / 5 Years); amenities carousel with caption tabs; testimonial on an orange panel beside a photo; full-screen menu as an orange panel listing sections in caps

## Signature move
Orange full-height menu panel + small square orange arrow buttons.

## Borrow
Fun stat ('20,000+ coffee cups consumed') to add personality.

## Don't copy
The stock photos.
