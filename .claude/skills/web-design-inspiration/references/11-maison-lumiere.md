# 11 · Maison Lumière: luxury homewares product page

**Family:** Quiet Luxury · **Best for:** homewares, furniture, ceramics, boutiques, premium single-product stores
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/26872277-E-commerce-Product-Page-for-a-Luxury-Retail-Brand (Ronas IT | UI/UX Team)
**Look at:** `images/11-maison-lumiere-hero.jpg` (product page), `images/11-maison-lumiere-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Whisper-quiet luxury. Limestone, linen and soft daylight.

## Palette (roles)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Page | warm limestone `#E7E0CD` (on a plaster texture backdrop) |
| Ink | `#29231B` / black |
| Controls | pure `#FFFFFF` flat rectangles |
| Photo tones | `#CCBE9C`, `#898376`, `#61594D` |

## Type
- **Helvetica regular only.** Product names in wide-tracked UPPERCASE ("PRESTIGE CERAMIC ART"), with tiny 12–13px body.
- Logo in a classic book serif ("Maison Lumière").
- Free fonts: **Inter 400** (uppercase, `0.02em`) + **Cormorant Garamond** or **EB Garamond** for the wordmark.

## Layout & components
- **Bracketed micro-labels**: `[About The Product]`, `[New Collection]`, `[Floor lamp]`.
- Controls are tiny flat white rectangles with no radius and no shadow: "Cart [0]", "Add to cart".
- Nav stacked as 3 short lines in the centre-top.
- Product page: big square photo left (~55%), title top-right, copy anchored low, vertical thumbnail rail on the far right.
- "You may be interested" row of tall interior-photo cards with caption overlays and category tabs.

## Signature move
Bracketed labels with microscopic flat controls. Restraint as the whole brand.

## Borrow
- Anchoring copy to the bottom of its column so whitespace sits on top.
- Thumbnail rails instead of carousel dots.
- A three-colour brand system (white / stone / black).

## Don't copy
The Maison Lumière name or its product photography.
