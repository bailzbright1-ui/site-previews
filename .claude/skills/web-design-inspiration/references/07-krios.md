# 07 · Krios: energy infrastructure platform

**Family:** Dark Tech + Soft Light · **Best for:** energy, infrastructure, climate, enterprise B2B
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27492729-Energy-Infrastructure-Website-Design-Krios (Phenomenon Labs for Phenomenon Studio)
**Look at:** `images/07-krios-sheet.jpg` (dark hero + all frames), `images/07-krios-detail.jpg` (hub diagram + map)

## Feel
Serious infrastructure made approachable. It opens dark and cinematic, then turns soft lavender light for the content.

## Palette (roles)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Hero | near-black `#060211` with electric violet glow |
| Brand | violet `#562ABF` / deep `#2D0B9A` |
| Content sections | lavender-white gradient `#F6F3FF` → `#DDD7E2` |
| Warm pop | lemon yellow pill (Contact Us, active tab), the only warm colour |

## Type
- Clean geometric sans, medium weight, sentence case. Calm and corporate.
- Eyebrows: "● 02 / REGIONAL FOOTPRINT" (dot + number + small caps).
- Free fonts: **Manrope** or **Figtree** (500/600) + **Inter**.

## Layout & components
- Dark hero with a glossy 3D violet glass ribbon and a short headline bottom-left.
- **Photos cropped into organic metaball / peanut blob masks** that connect along a thin line, like a process path.
- Hub-and-spoke diagram: a central logo tile in a circle, with app-icon-style tiles (Utilities / Datacenters / Landowners) on the spokes.
- Choropleth map (Europe) in violet tints for the region footprint.
- Pill tab group with one yellow active tab.
- Violet pill CTA paired with a separate circular arrow button.

## Signature move
Blob-masked photography on a connecting path, plus a hub diagram that explains the business model in one picture.

## Borrow
- Two-temperature pages: a dark hero, then light content in a tint of the same hue.
- Diagram-as-hero for businesses that connect parties (marketplaces, brokers, platforms).
- A service-area map in brand tints (great for local businesses).

## Don't copy
The Krios name or logo, or the violet ribbon render.
