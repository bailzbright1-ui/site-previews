# 53 · Furniture Store Website Concept

**Family:** Product Spotlight · **Best for:** furniture, appliances, mattresses, e-bikes, mowers, single hero product shops
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/25044281-Furniture-Store-Website-Concept (Bato)
**Look at:** `images/53-lento-furniture-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Bright, tactile and shoppable. The product is the hero.

## Palette (roles)
white + a soft product-matched tint panel (dusty aqua #9FCAD0 / #6EBFC9) for the right half; ink black; colour swatches in the product's options

## Type
heavy rounded geometric sans product name ('Lento Lounge Nest Chair'); free: Poppins 800 + Poppins

## Layout & components
split hero: product name, description, black 'Add to Cart' + outline 'Learn More' + heart, colour swatch dots on the left; product cut-out on a tinted panel on the right with CALLOUT LABELS on leader lines ('Soft velvet fabric', 'Solid oak wood'); bottom stat bar (2,5k+ Sold / 98% Satisfaction / 1,2k Reviews / 10 Year Warranty) ending in a tinted 'Buy Now USD $798' block

## Signature move
Annotated product with leader-line callouts + a stats bar that ends in the buy button.

## Borrow
Leader-line feature callouts on any product or equipment photo (a mower, an AC unit, a pool pump).

## Don't copy
The product photo.
