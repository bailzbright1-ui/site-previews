# 18 · Cleanora Cleaning Service Website Design

**Family:** Friendly Local Service · **Best for:** cleaning, maid, laundry, carpet cleaning, home services
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/26644176-Cleanora-Cleaning-Service-Website-Design (Oripio)
**Look at:** `images/18-cleanora-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Bright, upbeat and trustworthy. Fresh-smelling as a website.

## Palette (roles)
page #F0F1EA soft cream; ink #0B0A07; highlight panels in a lime-to-yellow gradient (#E3F25A to #F6D63A); CTA pill #F4D32D; small green #399653 for ticks

## Type
friendly neo-grotesk sentence-case headings + a HUGE high-contrast serif wordmark ('Cleanora') overlapping the hero photo; free: Fraunces or DM Serif Display for the wordmark + Plus Jakarta Sans

## Layout & components
hero photo with the headline in a gradient glass chip top-left and the giant serif brand word sitting across the bottom of the photo; stats row (10K+ / 100K / 4.9/5 / 15yrs); service cards with carousel progress bar; full-yellow 'How it works' section with numbered step cards beside a photo; testimonial grid

## Signature move
Giant serif brand word laid across the lower third of the hero photo, partly behind the person.

## Borrow
Gradient highlight chip for the headline; whole section flooded with the brand colour for the process steps.

## Don't copy
The Cleanora name or its stock photos.
