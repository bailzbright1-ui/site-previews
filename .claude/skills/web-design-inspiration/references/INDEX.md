## Shared DNA (what makes all of them look expensive)
1. **One idea per section**, with lots of air around it. None of them crams.
2. **Strict palettes**: 1 base + 1 ink + at most 1–2 accents. The accent covers under 5% of the page.
3. **Extreme type scale contrast**: display at 64–200px next to body at 13–16px, and very little in between.
4. **Two or three type styles total**, used with discipline (e.g. display, body, and one micro-label style).
5. **Consistent image art direction**: the same light, temperature and crop logic across the site.
6. **One memorable signature move** that people would describe to a friend.
7. **Small, precise UI**: pills, hairlines, small icons in circles, tiny uppercase labels.
8. **Real-feeling content**: specific numbers, names, prices and steps, never lorem ipsum.
