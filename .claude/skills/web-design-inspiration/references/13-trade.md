# 13 · Trade: investment app landing page

**Family:** Dark Tech Premium · **Best for:** fintech apps, trading, crypto, any app-download landing page
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27457724-Trade-Investment-Landing-Page (Emote Agency)
**Look at:** `images/13-trade-hero.jpg` (floating-card hero), `images/13-trade-sheet.jpg` (frames from the scroll video)

## Feel
Night-mode confidence with a soft violet glow. The product UI is the decoration.

## Palette (roles)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Base | near-black `#0E0E10` with a soft radial violet glow behind the centre |
| Accent | lavender / periwinkle `#9F8CFF` (headline word, buttons, full CTA panel), deeper `#603AFC` |
| 3D | glossy pink crystals and spheres `#E7A0C8` on the lavender CTA |
| Data | green `#3FBF6E` for up, red `#E5484D` for down |

## Type
- Display: heavy rounded geometric grotesk, with a small grey lead-in line above the giant word ("Invest with" / **Full Control**).
- Free fonts: **Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold** or **Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold** + **Inter**.

## Layout & components
- **The giant headline is surrounded by an orbit of floating mini UI cards** (FX pair + sparkline, price chip with % button, news card with photo, "XPT Alert") at different sizes and depths, overlapping the letters.
- KPI tiles row ($1,240 / 24 / 10K+ / +12%).
- A marquee of brand-logo "app icon" squares.
- Phone mockups scroll through real app states (watchlist, buy sheet).
- A full-bleed lavender CTA panel with glossy 3D crystals ("Invest on Your Terms", "Markets in Your Pocket") and app-store pills.

## Signature move
A word orbited by floating product cards, with parallax depth on scroll.

## Borrow
- Showing real product UI fragments as the hero decoration (works for any app or dashboard).
- A soft radial glow behind the focal point instead of a flat black.
- KPI tiles as social proof.

## Don't copy
The Trade name, stock logos or the 3D crystal assets.
