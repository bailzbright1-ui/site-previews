# 15 · Northshore Realty: real-estate agency

**Family:** Editorial Heritage · **Best for:** real-estate agencies, property listings, interior designers, clinics, consultancies
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/26909351-Real-Estate-Agency-Website-Design (Ronas IT | UI/UX Team)
**Look at:** `images/15-northshore-hero.jpg` (hero + search dock + stat circles), `images/15-northshore-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Calm, editorial and trustworthy. A magazine about homes.

## Palette (roles)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Page | `#F8F8F8` / white |
| Ink | `#161613` |
| Photo sky | teal `#143949` → pale sky |
| Stat circles | `#F3F3F1` pale grey |

## Type
- **Instrument Serif** for every heading, with **one word or phrase switched to italic** ("Find the *right home* for your lifestyle", "Our *services*", "Best *properties*").
- Small clean sans for body (13–15px).
- Free fonts: **Instrument Serif** + **Inter** (the designer's own brand sheet uses Instrument Serif).

## Layout & components
- Hero: a low-angle architecture photo against open sky, with the headline top-left in white.
- **A frosted translucent search dock on the bottom edge of the hero**: four fields, each with a white circular icon (Location / Price / Rooms / Property type), and a black "Search" pill.
- Header: logo left, links centre, a location chip (outline) + white "Log in" pill on the right.
- **Stats set inside huge pale-grey circles that touch edge to edge** (980 / 1,300 / 24 / 85) in serif numerals.
- An aerial city photo with floating price-pin cards on properties.
- Service cards as four photo tiles with tiny captions. Underline tabs for property types. The blog as four columns of headline + date with hairline rules.

## Signature move
The italic-word serif headline system, plus a search dock fused to the hero image.

## Borrow
- One italic emphasis per heading as a consistent brand voice.
- Search, quote or booking forms docked into the hero instead of a separate section.
- Stats in shapes (circles, pills) rather than plain numbers.

## Don't copy
The Northshore name, the Vancouver content or the specific photos.
