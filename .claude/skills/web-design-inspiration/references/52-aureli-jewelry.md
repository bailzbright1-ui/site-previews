# 52 · Luxury Jewelry Website

**Family:** Editorial Heritage · **Best for:** jewellers, watch shops, bridal, perfume, premium gifts, boutique fashion
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27672401-Luxury-Jewelry-Website (Aminur Rahman)
**Look at:** `images/52-aureli-jewelry-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Rich, romantic and classic retail. Gold on oxblood.

## Palette (roles)
deep oxblood/burgundy #6B0F1A panels; ivory #EEE9E0 page; black promo bar and dark banner; gold from product photography #DD9C5D

## Type
condensed caps statements in white on burgundy ('MADE FOR LIFE'S KEY MOMENTS'), centred serif-caps wordmark; small sans; free: Oswald 500 or Barlow Condensed + Cormorant Garamond for the logo + Inter

## Layout & components
announcement bar; split hero: burgundy text block with a gold ring icon + CTA beside a portrait wearing jewellery; best-sellers grid of products on white tiles; category tiles of model close-ups; burgundy story block with hands photo; review cards with star ratings and burgundy accent squares; dark full-width banner 'Find the piece you'll treasure forever'

## Signature move
Solid oxblood text panels paired edge-to-edge with portrait photography.

## Borrow
Announcement bar for offers; jewel-tone colour block as the brand's luxury signal.

## Don't copy
The Aureli name or photos.
