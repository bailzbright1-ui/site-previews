## Feel
Airy, romantic and refined. A wedding album with lots of white space.

## Palette (roles)
soft ivory #EFE8EA / #F7F4F0 page; warm earth photography (meadow greens, saddle browns #4E2D1A); grey-taupe text #989496

## Type
ultra-thin high-contrast serif, often in spaced caps ('TIMELESS ROMANCE OF THE AMERICAN WEST', 'Natural Beauty, Modern Elegance.'); tiny caps labels; free: Cormorant Garamond 300 + Montserrat 400 (tracking 0.2em)

## Layout & components
centred thin serif headline over a wide landscape photo; offset photo + text pairs with lots of empty ivory; mix of colour and B&W photos; small outlined 'About us' button; signature experience block ('Signature Horse Release') with photo right

## Signature move
Extreme whitespace + hairline-thin serif.

## Borrow
Let photos breathe with offset placement; B&W/colour mix for storytelling.

## Don't copy
The photos.
