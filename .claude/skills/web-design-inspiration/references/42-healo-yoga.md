## Feel
Soft, warm and nurturing. Blush and clay tones.

## Palette (roles)
blush/peach background #E9DDD9 / #F2C1AE; clay brown #945D41 pills and CTA; terracotta #E67A5A accents; ink #16120D

## Type
elegant serif headlines centred ('Inhale Confidence / Harmony in Every Pose'); small sans body; free: Cormorant Garamond 500 or Lora + DM Sans

## Layout & components
centred hero headline with two pill CTAs, then a row of ARCH-topped photo frames each holding a different pose; class schedule table (days x times) with dark header; pricing plans with photos; FAQ accordion; stat chips (500+ / 85+ / 24/7 / 100%) in white rounded tiles

## Signature move
Row of arch-shaped photo frames under a centred serif headline.

## Borrow
Weekly timetable grid for any class-based business; arches as a soft brand shape.

## Don't copy
The Healo name or photos.
