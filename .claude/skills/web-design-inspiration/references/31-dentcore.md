# 31 · Dentcore Dental Clinic Website Solution

**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27041190-Dentcore-Dental-Clinic-Website-Solution (Emon)

## Feel
Clean, confident and modern-clinical without being cold.

## Palette (roles)
white + cool grey #EDEEEF; vivid royal blue #0026CC as a full section colour; ink #18100F; photography of smiling staff/patients

## Type
light-to-regular neo-grotesk with ONE italic word in the headline ('Beautifully *smiles* / *Dental* treatment'); free: Inter Tight 300/400 italic + Inter

## Layout & components
hero photo in a blue frame with the headline across it; floating 3D tooth render; blue section with a huge stacked list of treatments in pale blue where the hovered/active one turns white and underlined (Teeth Whitening); team photo with about copy; numbered testimonials (01); giant pale brand word near the footer; navy footer

## Signature move
Treatments list as a huge stacked type index on blue with one active item.

## Borrow
The stacked-index service list (works for any clinic or trade with many services).

## Don't copy
The Dentcore name or stock photos.
