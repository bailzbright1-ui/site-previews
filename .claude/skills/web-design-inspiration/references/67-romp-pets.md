# 67 · Romp Pet Care Website UI Design Motion

**Family:** Loud Pop · **Best for:** pet food subscriptions, dog walkers, doggy daycare, kids' brands, subscription boxes
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27799015-Romp-Pet-Care-Website-UI-Design-Motion (Sayeed Ahmad)
**Look at:** `images/67-romp-pets-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Joyful, bouncy and bold. A brand with a wagging tail.

## Palette (roles)
electric periwinkle/indigo #3B3BF5 and lavender #B7B3F5; acid lime #D8F33A for blobs, badges and highlights; navy #031B58 ink; white

## Type
heavy rounded grotesk lowercase brand word set HUGE over the hero photo ('go romp'), with the dog running in front of the letters; Bricolage Grotesque (designer's own choice) for everything; period-ended statements ('Daily Bowl.', 'Everything they need. Nothing they don't.')

## Layout & components
hero photo of a running dog in front of a giant white wordmark, a lime hand-drawn LEASH LINE looping across the page, scalloped white cloud edge at the bottom; 4.9 rating badge; size selector chips (Small / Medium / Large) and a price-per-day plan builder; lime circle badges ('70% real meat'); service list (Walks / Grooming / Day care / Vet check) with arrows; brand sheet with colour bars

## Signature move
A hand-drawn looping line (leash) that travels across sections.

## Borrow
One doodled line motif that connects the page; plan builder with chips + price/day.

## Don't copy
The Romp name or photos.
