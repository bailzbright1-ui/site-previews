## Feel
Glowy, sensory and minimal. Light passing through the product.

## Palette (roles)
warm white #FCFAF7; amber/terracotta glow from photography (#D1935D, #AA5937); ink #1B0C09; blush backgrounds #E7C1A3

## Type
medium neo-grotesk, sentence case, tight ('Pure skincare that works with your skin'); stacked small nav list; free: Inter Tight 500 + Inter

## Layout & components
white card layout: left column with logo, vertical nav list, headline and two outline pill CTAs with ↗; right is a large rounded photo of a backlit product held in hand, slide counter + progress line at the bottom, a small glass product card (thumbnail, name, price, arrow) in the corner; pill Search and Bag buttons

## Signature move
Backlit glowing product photo with a floating mini product card.

## Borrow
Slide counter + thin progress line instead of dots; mini product card overlay on a lifestyle image.

## Don't copy
The brand names or photos.
