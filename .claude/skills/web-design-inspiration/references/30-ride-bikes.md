# 30 · Bicycle E commerce Website Design Modern Bike Shop UI UX

**Family:** Product Spotlight · **Best for:** bike shops, e-bikes, outdoor gear, power tools, mowers, any single-hero-product retailer
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27849249-Bicycle-E-commerce-Website-Design-Modern-Bike-Shop-UI-UX (Sudarsan Roy)
**Look at:** `images/30-ride-bikes-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Sporty and product-proud. Each model gets its own stage.

## Palette (roles)
light grey #F7F9F9 stage; per-product accent bands (cyan + lime, orange + black, red + black) taken from the product's own colours; ink #110D0C

## Type
giant pale model-name word in condensed caps BEHIND the product (BTWIN / SANTA / FOCUS); bold sans for band headlines; free: Big Shoulders Display 900 for the ghost word + Archivo

## Layout & components
product cut-out centred over its giant ghost name; model + price on a thin line under the product; full-width two-colour band split by a diagonal with headline left and copy + 'Shop now' pill right; minimal nav with phone pill

## Signature move
Giant ghost model name behind a product cut-out, with a diagonal two-colour band below.

## Borrow
Retheme the accent per product/service so each slide has its own colour story.

## Don't copy
The bike brand names.
