# 54 · Furniture Store Website Design Kasaform E commerce

**Family:** Moody Editorial · **Best for:** furniture, interior stores, homewares, architects, design studios, boutique hotels
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27852392-Furniture-Store-Website-Design-Kasaform-E-commerce (Yscale Studio)
**Look at:** `images/54-kasaform-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Slow, warm and curated. A design magazine with a soft voice.

## Palette (roles)
deep green-black #1A2420 sections; sage #98A58B and oat #E6DED1 sections; terracotta/rust #C6643A for the italic accent words; ink #080806

## Type
light sans headlines where the FIRST word is an italic coral serif ('*quiet* forms, lasting comfort — made for the way you live', '*layered* living', '*visit* us — sit, touch'); a big italic serif wordmark in the footer; free: Instrument Serif Italic + Inter 300

## Layout & components
alternating dark and light full-width panels; slightly rotated photo frames; product trio with a central circular image; cross-shaped photo collage around a small circular label; store visit card with a circular shopfront photo; huge italic 'kasaform' wordmark footer; round arrow controls

## Signature move
First word of every headline in a coloured italic serif.

## Borrow
Cross/plus-shaped photo collage; consistent 'italic first word' voice.

## Don't copy
The Kasaform name or photos.
