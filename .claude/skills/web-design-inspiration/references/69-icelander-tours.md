## Type

full-bleed misty mountain photo; thin crosshair hairlines dividing the hero into a grid (one horizontal, one vertical) with copy placed in a quadrant; stacked two-line logo top-left; currency selector top-right; social links bottom-right; headline bottom-left next to a tiny human figure for scale
