# 35 · Aesthetic Clinic Website Design Atelier Aesthetica

**Family:** Quiet Luxury · **Best for:** cosmetic clinics, plastic surgeons, skin clinics, day spas, private practices, boutique hotels
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27705580-Aesthetic-Clinic-Website-Design-Atelier-Aesthetica (Phenomenon Studio)
**Look at:** `images/35-atelier-aesthetica-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Hushed, expensive and reassuring. Warm taupe calm.

## Palette (roles)
warm taupe/mushroom #6D6051 / #BCAC98 bands; ivory #FFFEF6 content panels; espresso #241D17 ink; no bright accent

## Type
light neo-grotesk mixed with an elegant ITALIC SERIF word in the same headline ('Discuss Your Goals in *Confidence*', 'Where Surgical Experience Meets *Individualized Care*', '*Dr.* Alexandra Morel'); free: Inter 300 + Instrument Serif Italic

## Layout & components
centred headline with small portrait photos tucked into gaps between words; numbered process steps (01/04) with one interior photo each; specialist card: large portrait cut-out on taupe with name, credentials as tiny chips, arrow controls; big light stat '2,400+ Procedures' over a corridor photo

## Signature move
Small portrait photos tucked into the negative space around a centred headline.

## Borrow
Sans + italic serif within one headline; credentials as micro chips on a practitioner card.

## Don't copy
The Atelier Aesthetica name or photos.
