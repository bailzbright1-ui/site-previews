# 56 · Accounting Firm Website Design for Tax Advisory Services

**Family:** Editorial Heritage · **Best for:** accountants, tax agents, bookkeepers, financial planners, law firms, consultancies
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27876749-Accounting-Firm-Website-Design-for-Tax-Advisory-Services (Habibullah Mahmud)
**Look at:** `images/56-cohen-accounting-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Established yet personable. A business newspaper with warmth.

## Palette (roles)
white page; deep navy #131A2A for buttons, bars and dark bands; burnt orange #C6542F for the ampersand, italic emphasis and thin vertical rules; fresh leaf-green photography

## Type
condensed editorial SERIF headline with an orange ITALIC SERIF phrase ('National-level expertise, hands-on partners *& personal attention.*'); mono caps breadcrumb tags in the hero; free: Newsreader or Source Serif 4 (italic) + Inter

## Layout & components
announcement bar with 'Get the guides'; serif nav logo; three tag chips above the headline (TAX · ADVISORY / ...); headline left with navy CTA + text link; nature macro photo right with small label tags ('TAX', 'AUDIT') on leader lines; accordion service list; resource card overlapping the photo; navy stats band (15 / 3 / 19); featured insights with italic word

## Signature move
Orange italic serif ampersand phrase + photo with labelled leader-line tags.

## Borrow
Lead-magnet resource card (tax guide, checklist) in the hero; serif + italic voice for professional services.

## Don't copy
The Cohen & Co name or photos.
