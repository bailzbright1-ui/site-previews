# 33 · Tenderline Vet Veterinary Website Design

**Family:** Moody Editorial · **Best for:** vets, pet clinics, pet food, premium pet services, also any caring service that wants to feel premium
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27807289-Tenderline-Vet-Veterinary-Website-Design (Juan Pablo Devis)
**Look at:** `images/33-tenderline-vet-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Cinematic, tender and premium. A magazine cover for a vet clinic.

## Palette (roles)
midnight navy #0B1530 / near-black #06060A; warm cream #F3ECE3 sections; acid lime-yellow #D9F32C for the headline emphasis and CTAs; terracotta #B4533C as a secondary card colour

## Type
elegant book SERIF large ('Care, closer.' with the second word in lime); tiny wide-tracked uppercase labels; one handwritten-style italic script line; free: Newsreader or Cormorant + Inter (uppercase tracking 0.2em)

## Layout & components
full-bleed dramatic dog portrait hero with thin line-art anatomy annotations over the photo; corner registration marks and side labels; two glass 'Preventive / Urgent' mini cards; services as organic blob-shaped cards with sketch illustrations; 3-step numbered row (Tell us / Meet your vet / Make a plan) on cream; alternating photo + serif statement bands; lime CTA pills

## Signature move
Line-art annotations drawn over a cinematic hero photo.

## Borrow
Editorial serif + one acid accent on dark for a premium take on a 'friendly' industry.

## Don't copy
The Tenderline name or photos.
