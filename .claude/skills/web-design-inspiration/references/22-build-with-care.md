# 22 · Construction Company Website Design Landing Page

**Family:** Brutal Mono + Electric · **Best for:** construction, builders, civil, demolition, steel fabrication, industrial trades
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/25983467-Construction-Company-Website-Design-Landing-Page (Perevoz Design)
**Look at:** `images/22-build-with-care-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Gritty, heavy-duty and proud. A poster for builders.

## Palette (roles)
black #060607 base; electric ultramarine #0706F2 as the only colour; white type; all photography desaturated to black & white

## Type
heavy condensed ALL CAPS (BUILD / OUR TEAM / SERVICES) + giant OUTLINED caps words in blue behind sections (ABOUT US, OFFER); free: Archivo Black / Archivo ExtraCondensed + Archivo

## Layout & components
hero is a blue block with a B&W crane photo duotoned into it and a giant white 'BUILD' cut by the photo, then 'with care' in lowercase rounded bold; team row of tightly cropped B&W portraits; services as B&W photo tiles with caps labels; outlined giant words layered behind content; abstract blue geometric quarter-circle logo marks as section ornaments

## Signature move
Blue duotone photography + giant outlined section words behind content.

## Borrow
Converting all stock photos to B&W/duotone makes cheap photos look art-directed; outlined ghost words as section labels.

## Don't copy
The specific photos or logo mark.
