Questions

The fine print, in plain English.

Who owns my domain?

You do. If you already have one, we use it. If not, we can register one in your name. You pay the registration fee (the registrar's fee, at cost), separate from your plan. If you ever cancel, you keep the domain and we give you a copy of the site files.

Is there a contract?

No. Keep and Grow are month to month. Cancel any time and the next month just doesn't happen.

Can I change things on the site?

Yes. Send us what you'd like changed (wording, photos, prices, hours) and we'll do it. Keep includes 1 change a month, Grow up to 3.

Can I use my own photos?

Please do. Send us photos of your work and we'll swap them in before your site goes live.

What if I don't want it?

No worries. The preview comes down within 14 days and you won't hear from us again.

How fast can it go live?

Usually within the week, once your domain is connected. We send a test enquiry to your inbox so you know the form works.
