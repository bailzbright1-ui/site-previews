# 05 · WaveAI: trend forecasting web

**Family:** Loud Pop · **Best for:** AI and consumer tech, youth brands, events, launches
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27425155-Trend-Forecasting-Web (Emote Agency)
**Look at:** `images/05-waveai-hero.jpg`, `images/05-waveai-sheet.jpg` (frames from the scroll video)

## Feel
Loud, playful and a little cheeky. A poster that scrolls.

## Palette (roles)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Page | `#FFFFFF` |
| Ink | `#030303` |
| Signal | yellow ≈ `#FFCC00` (rounded panels) |
| Pop | tomato red `#E21806` (shapes, red headline variants) |
| Neutral | light grey `#EDEDED` step cards |

## Type
- Display: ultra-condensed heavy ALL-CAPS, **set edge to edge so one word fills the viewport width** ("FORECASTS", the footer wordmark).
- Micro: uppercase **monospace wrapped in parentheses** for nav and labels: `( PRODUCT ) ( ABOUT ) ( PRICING )`, `+ ( MENU )`.
- Free fonts: **Anton** or **Bebas Neue** + **JetBrains Mono** / **Space Mono**.

## Layout & components
- A phone mockup overlaps and cuts into the giant word, breaking the baseline.
- Chunky black and red flower, heart and blob shapes scattered on the yellow panel.
- Vertical rotated ticker text along the panel edges.
- Typographic play: "WHAT AI BUN *( [mascot] ) DO" puts an image inside parentheses as part of the sentence.
- Numbered 01 / 02 / 03 step cards on light grey.
- Footer: the brand name at full viewport width.

## Signature move
A word as wide as the screen with the product breaking through it, plus a 3D mascot with attitude.

## Borrow
- Viewport-width words (size them with `clamp()` and `vw`, and check they don't overflow).
- Parenthesised mono labels as a tiny brand language.
- A character or mascot only if the brand can own one.

## Don't copy
The black bunny mascot, the WaveAI name or its flower shapes. Invent your own character or shape set.
