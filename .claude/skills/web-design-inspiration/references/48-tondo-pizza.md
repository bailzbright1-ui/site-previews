# 48 · Tondo Bold Pizza Restaurant Website UI UX Design

**Family:** Loud Pop · **Best for:** pizzerias, burger bars, takeaway, food trucks, bottle shops, casual dining
**Source:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27510732-Tondo-Bold-Pizza-Restaurant-Website-UI-UX-Design (Instaquirk)
**Look at:** `images/48-tondo-pizza-sheet.jpg`

## Feel
Loud, hungry and confident. Menu-board energy.

## Palette (roles)
warm cream-peach #FDDCAB gradient hero; tomato red #B32C0C / #E23A1C accent and ticker band; basil green #2E8B4A full menu section; mustard #F6C443 testimonials band; espresso #1E1410 dark band

## Type
ultra-condensed heavy caps with periods, one line in red ('BIG. / BOLD. / PERFECTLY / ROUND.'); free: Anton or Bebas Neue + Inter

## Layout & components
FULL PAGE: hero with exploding pizza product shot and red 'See the menu' + outline 'Book a table'; red ticker band of selling points separated by ✶ (48-hour dough ✶ Wood-fired ✶ San Marzano tomatoes); signature pizzas as three cards; full menu in two columns on green; ingredients row; 'How Tondo gets made' 01/02/03 on dark; photo grid; yellow reviews; red 'Come get a slice' contact form; each section a different flat colour

## Signature move
Every section is a different flat brand colour, separated by a ticker band.

## Borrow
Selling-point ticker band; full menu as a typographic two-column list.

## Don't copy
The Tondo name or photos.
